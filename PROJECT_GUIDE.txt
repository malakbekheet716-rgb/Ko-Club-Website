1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
================================================================================
The "KODE Sports Club Employee Hub" is a modern, private website (an intranet portal) 
designed exclusively for the employees, managers, and HR staff of KODE Sports Club.

It allows club staff to:
- Read daily club announcements and department news.
- Check club event schedules and a monthly interactive calendar.
- Access their department's private workspace and organizational chart.
- Download club policy documents, forms, and guides (PDFs, Word docs).
- Find internal phone numbers and contact emails for every department.
- Submit suggestions, ideas, or problems directly to management.
- Give peer recognition awards and points (Trophy Room gamification).
- Manage staff accounts, departments, and system roles via an Admin Panel.
- Enjoy an animated floating mascot companion ("KODE Buddy") as they work.
- Switch seamlessly between English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) with 12 color themes.

5. UNDERSTANDING THE DOCUMENTATION (.MD FILES)
================================================================================
Files ending in `.md` are "Markdown" files. They are simple text documents that 
can be read in any text editor, or viewed as nicely formatted documents with 
bold titles, bullet points, and tables.

1. PROJECT_MEMORY.md:
   - Think of this as the "Project's Long-Term Memory".
   - It lists every single architectural decision ever made (from Decision #1 to #20).
   - It lists all 4 initial employee accounts, available departments, and roles.
   - It gives developers a step-by-step guide on how to plug in a real backend database.

2. TEST.md:
   - The test report verifying that there are zero bugs or invisible texts.
   - Confirms that Light Mode and Dark Mode work across all 10 pages.
   - Confirms that English and Arabic translations are 100% complete.

3. AGENTS.md:
   - The "Rules of the House" for any AI coding tool.
   - Rule 1: Always read PROJECT_MEMORY.md before writing code.
   - Rule 2: Keep data logic in `apiService.js` and page drawing in `app.js`.
   - Rule 3: Always support Light and Dark modes.

6. UNDERSTANDING THE CODE FILES (HOW THE PORTAL IS BUILT)
================================================================================
The portal is built using the three core building blocks of the web:

1. HTML (index.html) -> The Structure
   - Defines where the sidebar sits, where the top search bar is, where the main 
     content area is, and where the popup windows (modals) appear.

2. CSS (css/styles.css) -> The Style & Appearance
   - Controls the "Glassmorphism" look (translucent frosted-glass cards).
   - Defines the 19 color variables (so changing a theme changes all buttons at once).
   - Handles the Light Mode (bright white glass) and Dark Mode (sleek cosmic dark).
   - Handles the Arabic mode (mirrors sidebar to right, switches font to Cairo).

3. JavaScript (js/app.js & js/apiService.js) -> The Behavior & Data
   - `apiService.js`: Stores the list of users, departments, news, and events.
   - `app.js`: When you click "Events", it grabs events from `apiService.js`, formats 
     them into HTML cards, and displays them on the screen instantly.

7. HOW THE 10 PORTAL MODULES WORK
================================================================================
When you are logged in, the left sidebar gives you access to 10 distinct sections:

1. 🏠 My KODE (Home):
   - Features the "Daily Feed Swipe Deck".
   - You can click "Next" or drag/swipe the main hero card horizontally to browse 
     today's top news and club updates.

2. 📰 News:
   - Displays official club announcements and department-specific notices.
   - Includes a "Comments" button on each news item to start discussions.

3. 📅 Events & Calendar:
   - Shows upcoming club seminars, workshops, and staff meetings.
   - Features a clean 7-day monthly calendar showing event dates with blue dots.

4. 👥 My Department:
   - A private workspace customized to your assigned department (e.g. Marketing).
   - Shows the department organizational chart (Managers, Team Leads, Colleagues).
   - Includes a private "Ask Your Department" helpdesk form.

5. 📁 Resources:
   - A library of official club files: Employee Handbook, Safety Procedures, 
     Marketing Guidelines, and Leave Request Forms.
   - Filterable by Policies, Procedures, and Forms.

6. 📇 Contacts:
   - An internal directory to quickly find the right person or team (HR, Tech Support, 
     Facilities, Food Safety, Security) without searching the whole organization.

7. ❓ FAQs:
   - Frequently asked questions with instant answers regarding leave, policies, 
     and club equipment.

8. 💬 Feedback:
   - Allows employees to submit suggestions, report problems, or propose workplace 
     improvements (with an optional "Submit anonymously" checkbox).
   - Includes a status tracker showing which suggestions are Under Review or In Progress.

9. 🏆 KODE Recognition (Trophy Room):
   - Gamification and peer awards portal.
   - Displays your total earned points in a prominent purple gradient banner.
   - Managers and Admins can click "+ Grant an Award" to reward a colleague with points.

10. 🛡️ Admin Panel (Admin & HR Management Hub):
    - Visible to all users (with full editing powers for Admin & HR accounts).
    - Top KPI cards showing live counts of Total Personnel, Active Departments, and Roles.
    - 3 Segmented Tabs:
      * Employees Tab: Search employees by name or email, filter by department, add new 
        staff, and change roles/departments on the fly.
      * Departments Tab: View all active departments and create new departments.
      * Roles Tab: View all active system roles and create new access roles.

9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
================================================================================

Q: Can I run this portal without an internet connection?
A: YES! All code, scripts, mascot graphics, and mock databases live directly inside 
   this folder. It works completely offline.

Q: Do I need to install Node.js, npm, or Python?
A: Python 3.x is recommended so `start.bat` can spin up the local server. However, 
   even if you have neither Python nor Node, `start.bat` will automatically fall back 
   to opening `index.html` directly in your web browser.

Q: How do I share this with a colleague?
A: Simply copy or zip the entire folder and send it to them. When they receive it, 
   all they have to do is double-click `start.bat`.

Q: Where is the data saved?
A: In-memory and in your browser's `localStorage`. When you add a new employee or 
   grant an award, it saves immediately to your browser session.

Q: How can a software developer connect a real database later?
A: The developer only needs to open `js/apiService.js` and replace the mock functions 
   with real `fetch()` calls to their backend API (FastAPI, Django, Express, Node). 
   They will NOT need to modify `js/app.js` or `index.html` at all.
